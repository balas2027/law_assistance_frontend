Notice Period

  • Your agreement stipulates a 3-month notice period for early termination by the tenant.
  • Standard Practice: In Bangalore, a 1 to 2-month notice period is more common for residential leases, though 3 months is legally permissible if mutually agreed upon.
  • Legal Standing: The Karnataka Rent Control Act, 1999 does not strictly cap notice periods for private agreements outside specific rent-controlled premises, relying instead on contract law principles.

Security Deposit

  • The agreement demands a 10-month rent equivalent as a security deposit.
  • Standard Practice: While historically common in Bangalore, there has been a significant shift. The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 (adopted in spirit by many states) recommends capping residential security deposits at 2 months' rent.
  • Risk Flag: A 10-month deposit is exceptionally high in the current market and ties up significant capital. You may want to negotiate this down to 3-5 months.
